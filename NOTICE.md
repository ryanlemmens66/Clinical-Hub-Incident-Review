## Review content

The application stores nothing. Saved audit files and exported DOCX documents,
however, contain the review as written — which may include patient-identifiable
clinical detail, staff names and operational timings. Those files are clinical
records and must be stored, transmitted and disposed of under organisational
policy, regardless of how the tool that produced them is licensed.

The privacy statement at the top of the application sets out what it does and
does not do with review data. It states plainly that those are application
privacy controls and design intentions, and do not represent formal security
accreditation, organisational approval or a guarantee of compliance. That
distinction is deliberate and should be preserved.
