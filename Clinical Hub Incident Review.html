Privacy and Data Handling

This tool operates locally and does not transmit review information over the internet. Information entered remains in the open tab and is not stored in local storage, session storage, cookies or a browser database.

Data is written to the device only when you save a review or generate a DOCX. Exported files may contain sensitive clinical information and must be handled in accordance with organisational policy. Pasted content may remain in device or Citrix clipboard history.
